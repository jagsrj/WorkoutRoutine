About Your Workout Journal

The Journal acts as your personal digital logbook. It automatically aggregates every exercise session you record in the Tracking tab and organizes it chronologically by date.

How to Use:
  1. Select a past or current date at the top of the Tracking tab.
  2. Log sets in pounds (lbs), delete unneeded rows, and tap Save.
  3. Delete any accidental entries directly in this Journal tab using the red button.
