About Your Workout Journal

The Journal aggregates every functional trainer session logged in Tracking and organizes it chronologically by date.

Progression Rules:
  • Progressive Overload: Increase weight stack pin levels or add reps every 1–2 weeks with controlled negatives.
  • Prioritize Compounds: Perform heavy cable presses, pulldowns, and squats early in the session.
  • Nutrition: Support muscle recovery with high protein intake.
