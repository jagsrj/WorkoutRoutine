6-Month Master Plan Overview

Structured Upper/Lower split designed for age 44 (82 kg) to optimize muscle growth, body recomposition, and joint longevity.

Phase Structure (24 Weeks)
  • Phase 1 (Weeks 1–8): Foundation & Structural Strength – Form mastery, tendon resilience, baseline consistency.
  • Phase 2 (Weeks 9–16): Hypertrophy & Progression – Increased volume & intensity to drive muscle size and fat loss.
  • Phase 3 (Weeks 17–24): Peak Strength & Recomposition – Safely push main lifts to finalize visual transformation.
Weekly Workout Schedule
  • Mon: Upper Body A
  • Tue: Lower Body & Core A
  • Wed: Rest / Active Recovery (Light Treadmill / Cardio)
  • Thu: Upper Body B
  • Fri: Lower Body & Core B
  • Sat (Optional): Cardio, Core & Arms / Mobility
  • Sun: Rest
Double Progression Rule
  1. Choose rep range (e.g., 8–10 reps).
  2. Start with weight handled with strict form for 8 reps.
  3. Keep weight constant until completing target reps across all 3 sets.
  4. Increase weight by 2.5 to 5 lbs and reset to 8 reps.
Goal: Bench Press 75 lbs ➔ 115–125 lbs in 6 months.
Nutrition & Macro Guidelines (82 kg)
  • Protein Target: 135 g – 160 g daily (1.6–2.0 g/kg). Lean proteins: chicken breast, fish, eggs, Greek yogurt, whey.
  • Caloric Focus: Maintenance or slight 200–300 kcal deficit for body recomposition.
  • Recovery: 7–8 hours quality sleep for testosterone support and reduced joint inflammation.
