🦍 How to do Back Squat (Barbell)

Works: Legs, Glutes, Core · Starting weight: about 95 lbs

Step by step

  1. Set the bar on your upper back in a rack. Step back, feet shoulder-width.
  2. Brace your core and sit down between your heels until thighs are parallel.
  3. Drive up, keeping your chest tall.

💡 Tip: Always set the safety bars in the rack.
