🧍 How to do Wall Sit

Works: Legs · No equipment · Timed exercise

Step by step

  1. Lean your back flat against a wall.
  2. Slide down until your knees are bent like a chair.
  3. Hold it there. Keep breathing!

💡 Tip: Not as low? That is fine — go only as low as feels OK.
