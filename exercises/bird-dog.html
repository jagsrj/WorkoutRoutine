🐕 How to do Bird Dog

Works: Core, Back · No equipment

Step by step

  1. Start on hands and knees.
  2. Reach one arm forward and the opposite leg back.
  3. Hold for 2 seconds, come back, switch.

💡 Tip: Pretend there is a cup of water on your back — do not spill it!
