🔄 How to do Crunches

Works: Abs · No equipment

Step by step

  1. Lie on your back, knees bent, hands by your ears.
  2. Curl your shoulders up off the floor using your tummy.
  3. Lower slowly.

💡 Tip: Don't pull on your neck. Imagine an apple under your chin.
