🚣 Bent-Over Row (Smith machine)

A home-gym version of Barbell Rows. Works: Back, Biceps.

Step by step

  1. Set the safety stoppers first so the bar can never come down on you.
  2. Set the bar at knee height. Hinge forward with a flat back and grab it.
  3. Pull the bar to your belly, squeezing your shoulder blades.
  4. Lower slowly.

💡 Tip: Keep your back flat the whole time.
