📐 Incline Press (Smith machine)

A home-gym version of Incline Bench Press (Barbell). Works: Upper Chest, Shoulders.

Step by step

  1. Set the safety stoppers first so the bar can never come down on you.
  2. Set the bench to a low incline under the bar.
  3. Grab the bar a bit wider than your shoulders and twist to unhook.
  4. Lower to your upper chest, then press up.

💡 Tip: A low incline (about 30°) works the upper chest best.
