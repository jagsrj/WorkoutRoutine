🦵 How to do Leg Press

Works: Legs, Glutes · Starting weight: about 90 lbs

Step by step

  1. Sit in the machine with feet shoulder-width on the platform.
  2. Unlock and lower the platform until knees are bent about 90°.
  3. Press back up without locking your knees.

💡 Tip: Keep your lower back pressed into the seat.
