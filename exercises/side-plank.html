📏 How to do Side Plank

Works: Core · No equipment · Timed exercise

Step by step

  1. Lie on your side, propped up on your forearm.
  2. Lift your hips so your body is straight.
  3. Hold, then switch sides.

💡 Tip: Bend your bottom knee on the floor to make it easier.
