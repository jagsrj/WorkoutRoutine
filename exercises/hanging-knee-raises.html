🧗 How to do Hanging Knee Raises

Works: Core · No equipment

Step by step

  1. Hang from a bar (or use the captain's chair).
  2. Bring your knees up towards your chest.
  3. Lower slowly without swinging.

💡 Tip: Can't hang yet? Do lying knee tucks on the floor.

In the KinFit plans

  • Intermediate plan, Lower Body B: 3 sets × 10–15 reps
