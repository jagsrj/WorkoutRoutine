🌪️ How to do Russian Twists

Works: Core, Obliques · No equipment

Step by step

  1. Sit with knees bent, lean back a little.
  2. Hold your hands together in front of your chest.
  3. Turn your shoulders side to side, touching the floor beside your hip.

💡 Tip: Keep feet on the floor until this feels easy.
