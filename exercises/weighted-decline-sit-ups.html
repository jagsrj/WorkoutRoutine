🔻 How to do Weighted Decline Sit-Ups

Works: Abs · Starting weight: about 10 lbs plate

Step by step

  1. Hook feet on a decline bench, hold a plate at your chest.
  2. Curl up until your chest is near your knees.
  3. Lower slowly.

💡 Tip: Curl your spine — don't just yank with your hips.
