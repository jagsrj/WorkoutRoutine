🚶 How to do Dumbbell Lunges

Works: Legs, Glutes · Starting weight: about 15 lbs each

Step by step

  1. Hold dumbbells at your sides.
  2. Step forward and lower until both knees are bent about 90°.
  3. Push back up and switch legs.

💡 Tip: Keep your front knee over your foot, not caving in.

In the KinFit plans

  • Intermediate plan, Lower Body B: 3 sets × 8–10 reps each leg
