🦸 How to do Superman

Works: Back, Glutes · No equipment

Step by step

  1. Lie on your tummy with arms stretched in front.
  2. Lift your arms, chest and legs a little off the floor.
  3. Hold for 2 seconds, then lower slowly.

💡 Tip: Look at the floor to keep your neck relaxed.
