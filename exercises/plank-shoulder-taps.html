👋 How to do Plank Shoulder Taps

Works: Shoulders, Core · No equipment

Step by step

  1. Get into a push-up position with straight arms.
  2. Lift one hand and tap the opposite shoulder.
  3. Put it back and switch. Try not to rock your hips.

💡 Tip: Spread your feet wider to make it easier.
