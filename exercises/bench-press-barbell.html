🏋️ How to do Bench Press (Barbell)

Works: Chest, Triceps, Shoulders · Starting weight: about 75 lbs

Step by step

  1. Lie on the bench, eyes under the bar, feet flat.
  2. Grip slightly wider than shoulders, unrack and lower to mid-chest.
  3. Press up and slightly back over your shoulders.

💡 Tip: Always use a spotter or safety pins when going heavy.
