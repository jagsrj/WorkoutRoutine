🦶 Dumbbell Calf Raise

A home-gym version of Calf Raises. Works: Calves.

Step by step

  1. Hold a dumbbell in each hand. Stand on a step with heels hanging off.
  2. Rise onto your tiptoes as high as you can.
  3. Lower your heels slowly below the step.

💡 Tip: Hold a wall with one hand for balance if needed.
