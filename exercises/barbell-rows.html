🚣 How to do Barbell Rows

Works: Back, Biceps · Starting weight: about 75 lbs

Step by step

  1. Hinge forward with a flat back, bar hanging at knee level.
  2. Pull the bar to your lower ribs.
  3. Lower under control.

💡 Tip: Torso stays still — no jerking up.

At home: other ways to do it

Bent-Over Row (Smith machine)
