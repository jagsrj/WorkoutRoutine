💪 How to do Barbell Curls

Works: Biceps · Starting weight: about 45 lbs

Step by step

  1. Stand holding the bar with palms up, shoulder-width.
  2. Curl to your chest with elbows still.
  3. Lower slowly.

💡 Tip: Use an EZ bar if your wrists feel strained.
