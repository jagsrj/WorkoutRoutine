🙇 How to do Romanian Deadlift (Barbell)

Works: Hamstrings, Glutes, Back · Starting weight: about 95 lbs

Step by step

  1. Hold the bar at hip height, soft knees.
  2. Push hips back, bar sliding down your thighs.
  3. Stop at mid-shin or when your back wants to round; stand up.

💡 Tip: Bar stays touching your legs the whole time.
