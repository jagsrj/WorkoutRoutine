↔️ How to do Side Lunges

Works: Legs, Glutes · No equipment

Step by step

  1. Stand with feet together.
  2. Take a big step to the side and sit back into that hip. Other leg stays straight.
  3. Push back to the middle and switch sides.

💡 Tip: Keep your chest up and your heel down.
