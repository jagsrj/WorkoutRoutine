💪 EZ Bar Curls

A home-gym version of Barbell Curls. Works: Biceps.

Step by step

  1. Stand tall holding the EZ bar on the angled grips, palms up.
  2. Curl it up, keeping your elbows by your sides.
  3. Lower slowly.

💡 Tip: No swinging.
