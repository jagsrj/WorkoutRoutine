🧗 How to do Weighted Pull-Ups

Works: Back, Biceps · Starting weight: about 10 lbs

Step by step

  1. Hang a weight from a belt or hold a dumbbell between your feet.
  2. Pull your chest up to the bar.
  3. Lower all the way down under control.

💡 Tip: Only add weight once you can do 10 clean pull-ups.
