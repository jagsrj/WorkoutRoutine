🦶 How to do Calf Raises

Works: Calves · Starting weight: about 65 lbs

Step by step

  1. Stand on the machine or a step holding weight.
  2. Rise up onto your toes as high as you can.
  3. Lower your heels below the step for a stretch.

💡 Tip: Pause at the top for one second.
