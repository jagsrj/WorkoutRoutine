🕊️ How to do Lateral Raises

Works: Shoulders · Starting weight: about 10 lbs each

Step by step

  1. Stand holding light dumbbells at your sides.
  2. Raise your arms out to the sides up to shoulder height.
  3. Lower slowly.

💡 Tip: Go light! Lead with your elbows like you are pouring water.
