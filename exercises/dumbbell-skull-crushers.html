💀 How to do Dumbbell Skull Crushers

Works: Triceps · Starting weight: about 12 lbs each

Step by step

  1. Lie on a bench holding dumbbells above your chest.
  2. Bend only your elbows to lower the weights beside your head.
  3. Straighten your arms to lift back up.

💡 Tip: Keep your upper arms still and pointing up.
