🦿 How to do Leg Extensions

Works: Quads · Starting weight: about 60 lbs

Step by step

  1. Sit in the machine with the pad on your lower shins.
  2. Straighten your legs fully and squeeze.
  3. Lower slowly.

💡 Tip: Hold the handles and keep your bottom on the seat.
