💥 How to do Burpees

Works: Whole body, Heart · No equipment

Step by step

  1. From standing, squat down and put your hands on the floor.
  2. Jump or step your feet back to a push-up position.
  3. Jump or step back in, then stand up and reach high.

💡 Tip: Step instead of jumping to make it easier.
