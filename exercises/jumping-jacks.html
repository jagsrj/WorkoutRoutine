⭐ How to do Jumping Jacks

Works: Whole body, Heart · No equipment

Step by step

  1. Stand tall, arms by your sides.
  2. Jump your feet out wide and swing your arms overhead.
  3. Jump back to the start. Keep a steady rhythm.

💡 Tip: Low-impact version: step one foot out at a time.
