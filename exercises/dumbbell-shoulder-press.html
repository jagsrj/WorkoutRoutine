🏋️ How to do Dumbbell Shoulder Press

Works: Shoulders, Triceps · Starting weight: about 15 lbs each

Step by step

  1. Sit tall on a bench with back support. Dumbbells at shoulder height.
  2. Press them up until arms are straight.
  3. Lower slowly back to your shoulders.

💡 Tip: Do not arch your lower back — squeeze your tummy.
