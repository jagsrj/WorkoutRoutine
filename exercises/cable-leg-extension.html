🦿 Cable Leg Extension

A home-gym version of Leg Extensions. Works: Quads.

Step by step

  1. Put an ankle strap on a low pulley. Sit on a bench facing away from the machine.
  2. Strap one ankle and hold the sides of the bench.
  3. Straighten your leg until it's almost straight and squeeze your thigh.
  4. Lower slowly. Do all the reps, then switch legs.

💡 Tip: Go slowly: no swinging.
