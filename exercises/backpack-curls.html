💪 How to do Backpack Curls

Works: Biceps · Uses weights

Step by step

  1. Hold a backpack by the handle (or two water bottles).
  2. Keep elbows tucked at your sides.
  3. Curl it up to your shoulders, then lower slowly.

💡 Tip: No swinging! Keep your body still.
