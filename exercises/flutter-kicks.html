🏊 How to do Flutter Kicks

Works: Core, Hip flexors · No equipment · Timed exercise

Step by step

  1. Lie on your back with legs straight.
  2. Lift your feet a little off the floor.
  3. Kick up and down in small, fast movements.

💡 Tip: Press your lower back into the floor. Hands under hips can help.
