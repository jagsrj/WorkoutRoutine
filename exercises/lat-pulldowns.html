⬇️ How to do Lat Pulldowns

Works: Back, Biceps · Starting weight: about 60 lbs

Step by step

  1. Sit at the machine with thighs under the pad. Grab the bar wide.
  2. Lean back a little and pull the bar to your upper chest.
  3. Squeeze your shoulder blades, then let the bar rise slowly.

💡 Tip: Pull with your elbows, not your hands.
