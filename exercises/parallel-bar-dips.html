🤸 How to do Parallel Bar Dips

Works: Chest, Triceps · No equipment

Step by step

  1. Hold the bars with straight arms.
  2. Lean slightly forward and lower until shoulders are just below elbows.
  3. Press back up.

💡 Tip: Use the assisted machine if needed. Stop if shoulders hurt.
