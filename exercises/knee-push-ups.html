🙌 How to do Knee Push-Ups

Works: Chest, Arms · No equipment

Step by step

  1. Kneel on the floor and put your hands a little wider than your shoulders.
  2. Keep your body straight from head to knees, like a plank.
  3. Lower your chest towards the floor, then push back up.

💡 Tip: Too hard? Do them standing, pushing against a wall.
