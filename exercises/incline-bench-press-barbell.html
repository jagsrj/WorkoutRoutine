📐 How to do Incline Bench Press (Barbell)

Works: Upper Chest, Shoulders · Starting weight: about 65 lbs

Step by step

  1. Set the bench at about 30°. Unrack the bar.
  2. Lower to your upper chest.
  3. Press up over your chin.

💡 Tip: Use a spotter or safety arms.
