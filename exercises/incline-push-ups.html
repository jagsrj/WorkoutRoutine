📐 How to do Incline Push-Ups

Works: Chest, Arms · No equipment

Step by step

  1. Put your hands on a bench, sturdy table or stairs.
  2. Walk your feet back until your body is straight.
  3. Lower your chest to the edge, then push away.

💡 Tip: The lower the surface, the harder it is.
