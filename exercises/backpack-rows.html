🎒 How to do Backpack Rows

Works: Back, Arms · Uses weights

Step by step

  1. Put a few books in a backpack. Hold it with both hands.
  2. Bend forward at your hips with a flat back and soft knees.
  3. Pull the bag up to your belly button, squeeze your back, then lower it slowly.

💡 Tip: Keep your back flat like a table — no rounding.
