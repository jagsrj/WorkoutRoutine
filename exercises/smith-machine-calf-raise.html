🦶 Smith Machine Calf Raise

A home-gym version of Calf Raises. Works: Calves.

Step by step

  1. Set the safety stoppers first so the bar can never come down on you.
  2. Stand under the bar on your toes (on a plate or step if you have one). Unhook it.
  3. Rise up onto your tiptoes as high as you can.
  4. Lower your heels slowly all the way down.

💡 Tip: Pause for one second at the top.
