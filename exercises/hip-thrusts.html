🍑 How to do Hip Thrusts

Works: Glutes, Hamstrings · Starting weight: about 45 lbs

Step by step

  1. Sit with your upper back against a bench, weight across your hips.
  2. Push through your heels to lift your hips until your body is flat.
  3. Squeeze your glutes at the top, then lower.

💡 Tip: Use a pad on the bar for comfort.
