🍑 Hip Thrust (Smith machine)

A home-gym version of Barbell Hip Thrust. Works: Glutes, Hamstrings.

Step by step

  1. Set the safety stoppers first so the bar can never come down on you.
  2. Sit with your upper back against the bench, bar over your hips (use a pad).
  3. Twist to unhook and drive your hips up until your body is flat like a table.
  4. Lower slowly.

💡 Tip: Chin tucked, squeeze your glutes at the top.
