📐 How to do Incline Dumbbell Press

Works: Upper Chest, Shoulders · Starting weight: about 20 lbs each

Step by step

  1. Set a bench at a low incline. Lie back with dumbbells at chest level.
  2. Press them up over your chest.
  3. Lower slowly until your elbows are just below the bench.

💡 Tip: Keep your feet flat on the floor.

In the KinFit plans
