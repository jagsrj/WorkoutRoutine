🐛 How to do Inchworms

Works: Whole body, Hamstrings · No equipment

main muscle helpers

Step by step

  1. Stand tall, then bend forward and put your hands on the floor.
  2. Walk your hands out to a push-up position.
  3. Walk your hands back to your feet and stand up.

💡 Tip: Bend your knees as much as you need.
