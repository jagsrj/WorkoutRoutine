🪜 How to do Step-Ups

Works: Legs, Glutes · No equipment

Step by step

  1. Stand in front of a low, sturdy step or the bottom stair.
  2. Step up with one foot and stand tall on top.
  3. Step down slowly. Do all reps, then switch legs.

💡 Tip: Push through the heel of the foot that is on the step.
