🧱 How to do Plank

Works: Core · No equipment · Timed exercise

Step by step

  1. Rest on your forearms and toes.
  2. Make a straight line from head to heels.
  3. Squeeze your tummy and breathe. Hold!

💡 Tip: Hips sagging? Drop to your knees and keep going.
