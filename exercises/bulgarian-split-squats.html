🦩 How to do Bulgarian Split Squats

Works: Legs, Glutes · Starting weight: about 20 lbs each

Step by step

  1. Stand in front of a bench, put one foot behind you on it.
  2. Lower straight down until your front thigh is parallel.
  3. Drive up through your front heel.

💡 Tip: Hold a wall for balance at first.
