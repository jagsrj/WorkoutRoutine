⬇️ Lat Pulldown (functional trainer)

A home-gym version of Lat Pulldowns. Works: Back, Biceps.

Step by step

  1. Put both pulleys at the top. Clip on a long bar, or use two handles.
  2. Kneel, or sit on a bench, between the towers. Grab wide.
  3. Pull down to your upper chest, squeezing your shoulder blades together.
  4. Let it go back up slowly until your arms are straight.

💡 Tip: Chest up and don't lean back too far.
