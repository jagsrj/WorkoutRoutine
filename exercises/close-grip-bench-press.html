🏋️ How to do Close-Grip Bench Press

Works: Triceps, Chest · Starting weight: about 65 lbs

Step by step

  1. Lie on the bench, hands shoulder-width on the bar.
  2. Lower to your lower chest, elbows close to your body.
  3. Press back up.

💡 Tip: Always use a spotter or safety pins.
