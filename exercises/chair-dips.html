🪑 How to do Chair Dips

Works: Triceps · No equipment

Step by step

  1. Sit on the edge of a sturdy chair that will not slide. Hands next to your hips.
  2. Slide your bottom off the chair, knees bent.
  3. Bend your elbows to lower a little, then push back up.

💡 Tip: Only go down a little at first. Keep shoulders away from your ears.
