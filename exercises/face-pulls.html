🎯 How to do Face Pulls

Works: Rear shoulders, Upper back · Starting weight: about 25 lbs

Step by step

  1. Set a rope at face height on a cable machine.
  2. Pull the rope towards your face, elbows high and wide.
  3. Squeeze your shoulder blades, then return slowly.

💡 Tip: Great for posture. Go light and smooth.
