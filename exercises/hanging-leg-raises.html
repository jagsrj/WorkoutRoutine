🧗 How to do Hanging Leg Raises

Works: Core · No equipment

main muscle helpers

Step by step

  1. Hang from a bar with straight arms.
  2. Lift straight legs up to hip height or higher.
  3. Lower slowly without swinging.

💡 Tip: Bend your knees if straight legs are too hard.
