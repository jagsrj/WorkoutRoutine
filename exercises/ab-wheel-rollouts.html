🛞 How to do Ab Wheel Rollouts

Works: Core · No equipment

Step by step

  1. Kneel holding an ab wheel under your shoulders.
  2. Roll forward slowly, keeping your back flat.
  3. Pull back with your abs.

💡 Tip: Only roll as far as you can without your back sagging.
