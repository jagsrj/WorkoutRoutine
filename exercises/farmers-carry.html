🧺 How to do Farmer's Carry

Works: Grip, Core, Shoulders · Starting weight: about 25 lbs each · Timed exercise

Step by step

  1. Pick up a heavy dumbbell in each hand.
  2. Stand tall with shoulders back.
  3. Walk with small, steady steps.

💡 Tip: Don't let the weights swing or your shoulders shrug.
