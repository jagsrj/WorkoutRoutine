🏋️ How to do Dumbbell Bench Press

Works: Chest, Triceps · Starting weight: about 20 lbs each

Step by step

  1. Lie on a bench with dumbbells at chest level.
  2. Press them up until your arms are straight.
  3. Lower slowly to the sides of your chest.

💡 Tip: Feet flat and shoulder blades squeezed together.
