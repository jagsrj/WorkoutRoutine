🔥 Intermediate workout plan

Dumbbells & machines. Build real strength. Four short workouts a week: two upper-body days and two lower-body days, with rest and active days in between.

Equipment: Dumbbells, cable machines, a bench. Rest between sets: about 90 seconds.

Is this level right for you?

  • You have exercised regularly for at least 2–3 months
  • You can do 6–20 push-ups with good form
  • You know how to use basic gym machines
  • You finished the Beginner plan and it feels easy

Your week

  • Mon: 💪 Upper Body A (Push & Pull)
  • Tue: 🦵 Lower Body A (Legs & Core)
  • Wed: 🚶 Active rest
  • Thu: 🏋️ Upper Body B (Chest, Back & Arms)
  • Fri: ⚡ Lower Body B (Legs & Balance)
  • Sat: ⭐ Bonus day
  • Sun: 😴 Rest day

💪 Upper Body A: Push & Pull

  1. 🙌Push-Ups
    3 sets × 8–15 reps
    Chest, Arms, Core
  2. ⬇️Lat Pulldowns
    3 sets × 10–12 reps
    Back, Biceps
  3. 🏋️Dumbbell Shoulder Press
    3 sets × 10–12 reps
    Shoulders, Triceps
  4. 💪Bicep Curls
    3 sets × 10–12 reps
    Biceps

🦵 Lower Body A: Legs & Core

  1. 🏆Goblet Squats
    3 sets × 10–12 reps
    Legs, Glutes, Core
  2. 🙇Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift
    3 sets × 10–12 reps
    Hamstrings, Glutes, Back
  3. 🦿Leg Extensions
    3 sets × 12–15 reps
    Quads
  4. 🧱Plank
    3 sets × 30–45 sec
    Core

🏋️ Upper Body B: Chest, Back & Arms

  1. 📐Incline Dumbbell Press
    3 sets × 10–12 reps
    Upper Chest, Shoulders
  2. 🚣Seated Cable Rows
    3 sets × 10–12 reps
    Back, Biceps
  3. 🕊️Lateral Raises
    3 sets × 12–15 reps
    Shoulders
  4. ⏬Tricep Pushdowns
    3 sets × 12–15 reps
    Triceps

⚡ Lower Body B: Legs & Balance

  1. 🚶Dumbbell Lunges
    3 sets × 8–10 reps each leg
    Legs, Glutes
  2. 🦵Leg Press
    3 sets × 10–12 reps
    Legs, Glutes
  3. 🦶Calf Raises
    4 sets × 12–15 reps
    Calves
  4. 🧗Hanging Knee Raises
    3 sets × 10–15 reps
    Core

How to progress

Start at the low end of each rep range with perfect form. When you can do the top of the range for every set, add a little weight or a harder version. The plan runs for 24 weeks in three phases (Foundation, Build, Strength), and every 4th week is a lighter recovery week.
