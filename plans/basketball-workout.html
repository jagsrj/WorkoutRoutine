🏀 Basketball workout

Jumping power and quick moves. No equipment needed, about 20 minutes. Do it 2–3 times a week alongside practice.

  1. ⭐Jumping Jacks
    3 sets × 20–30 reps
    Whole body, Heart
  2. 🪑Bodyweight Squats
    3 sets × 12–15 reps
    Legs, Glutes
  3. 🪜Step-Ups
    3 sets × 8–10 reps each leg
    Legs, Glutes
  4. 🦀Lateral Shuffle
    3 sets × 20–30 sec
    Legs, Agility
  5. 🦶Calf Raises
    3 sets × 15–20 reps
    Calves
  6. 💥Burpees
    3 sets × 6–10 reps
    Whole body, Heart
  7. 🧱Plank
    3 sets × 20–40 sec
    Core

Warm up for 5 minutes first (jog on the spot, arm circles, leg swings). Rest 45–60 seconds between sets.
