🏆 Expert workout plan

Barbells. Heavy lifts. Big goals. Four short workouts a week: two upper-body days and two lower-body days, with rest and active days in between.

Equipment: Barbell, squat rack, pull-up bar, full gym. Rest between sets: about 120 seconds.

Is this level right for you?

  • You have trained 3+ times a week for 6+ months
  • You can do 20+ push-ups and some pull-ups
  • You know how to squat, bench and deadlift safely
  • Adults only — or teens with a coach watching

Your week

  • Mon: 💪 Upper Body A (Push & Pull)
  • Tue: 🦵 Lower Body A (Legs & Core)
  • Wed: 🚶 Active rest
  • Thu: 🏋️ Upper Body B (Chest, Back & Arms)
  • Fri: ⚡ Lower Body B (Legs & Balance)
  • Sat: ⭐ Bonus day
  • Sun: 😴 Rest day

💪 Upper Body A: Push & Pull

  1. 🏋️Bench Press (Barbell)
    4 sets × 6–8 reps
    Chest, Triceps, Shoulders
  2. 🧗Pull-Ups
    4 sets × 5–10 reps
    Back, Biceps
  3. 🙆Overhead Press (Barbell)
    3 sets × 6–8 reps
    Shoulders, Triceps, Core
  4. 💪Barbell Curls
    3 sets × 8–10 reps
    Biceps

🦵 Lower Body A: Legs & Core

  1. 🦍Back Squat (Barbell)
    4 sets × 5–8 reps
    Legs, Glutes, Core
  2. 🙇Romanian Deadlift (Barbell)
    3 sets × 8–10 reps
    Hamstrings, Glutes, Back
  3. 🔻Weighted Decline Sit-Ups
    3 sets × 12–15 reps
    Abs
  4. 🦶Calf Raises
    4 sets × 12–15 reps
    Calves

🏋️ Upper Body B: Chest, Back & Arms

  1. 📐Incline Bench Press (Barbell)
    4 sets × 8–10 reps
    Upper Chest, Shoulders
  2. 🚣Barbell Rows
    4 sets × 8–10 reps
    Back, Biceps
  3. 🤸Parallel Bar Dips
    3 sets × 8–12 reps
    Chest, Triceps
  4. 🕊️Lateral Raises
    3 sets × 12–15 reps
    Shoulders

⚡ Lower Body B: Legs & Balance

  1. ⚡Deadlift (Barbell)
    3 sets × 3–5 reps
    Whole back side, Legs, Grip
  2. 🦩Bulgarian Split Squats
    3 sets × 8–10 reps each leg
    Legs, Glutes
  3. 🧗Hanging Leg Raises
    3 sets × 10–15 reps
    Core
  4. 🛞Ab Wheel Rollouts
    3 sets × 8–12 reps
    Core

How to progress

Start at the low end of each rep range with perfect form. When you can do the top of the range for every set, add a little weight or a harder version. The plan runs for 24 weeks in three phases (Foundation, Build, Strength), and every 4th week is a lighter recovery week.
