⚽ Soccer workout

Speed, agility and strong legs. No equipment needed, about 20 minutes. Do it 2–3 times a week alongside practice.

  1. 🏃High Knees
    3 sets × 20–30 sec
    Legs, Heart
  2. 🪑Bodyweight Squats
    3 sets × 12–15 reps
    Legs, Glutes
  3. ↔️Side Lunges
    3 sets × 8–10 reps each side
    Legs, Glutes
  4. 🌉Single-Leg Glute Bridge
    3 sets × 8–10 reps each leg
    Glutes, Hamstrings
  5. 🦶Calf Raises
    3 sets × 15–20 reps
    Calves
  6. 📏Side Plank
    3 sets × 20–30 sec each side
    Core
  7. 🦀Lateral Shuffle
    3 sets × 20–30 sec
    Legs, Agility

Warm up for 5 minutes first (jog on the spot, arm circles, leg swings). Rest 45–60 seconds between sets.
