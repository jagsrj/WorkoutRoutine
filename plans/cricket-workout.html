🏏 Cricket workout

Rotation power, quick feet, strong shoulders. No equipment needed, about 20 minutes. Do it 2–3 times a week alongside practice.

  1. 🏃High Knees
    3 sets × 20–30 sec
    Legs, Heart
  2. 🌪️Russian Twists
    3 sets × 10–12 reps each side
    Core, Obliques
  3. ↔️Side Lunges
    3 sets × 8–10 reps each side
    Legs, Glutes
  4. 🦵Reverse Lunges
    3 sets × 8–10 reps each leg
    Legs, Glutes
  5. 🦸Superman
    3 sets × 10–12 reps
    Back, Glutes
  6. 👋Plank Shoulder Taps
    3 sets × 8–12 reps each side
    Shoulders, Core
  7. 🦀Lateral Shuffle
    3 sets × 20–30 sec
    Legs, Agility

Warm up for 5 minutes first (jog on the spot, arm circles, leg swings). Rest 45–60 seconds between sets.
