✳️ How to do Plank Jacks

Works: Core, Heart · No equipment · Timed exercise

main muscle helpers

Step by step

  1. Start in a plank on straight arms.
  2. Jump your feet out wide, then back together.
  3. Keep your hips steady and your tummy tight.

💡 Tip: Step the feet out one at a time to make it easier.
