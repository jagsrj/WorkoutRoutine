🌅 How to do Good Morning (Barbell)

Works: Hamstrings, Lower back, Glutes · Starting weight: about 45 lbs

main muscle helpers

Step by step

  1. Rest a light bar on your upper back, feet hip-width.
  2. With soft knees and a flat back, push your hips back and lean forward.
  3. Squeeze your bottom to stand back up.

💡 Tip: Go light. Stop when you feel a stretch in your hamstrings.
