🦶 How to do Calf Raises

Works: Calves · No equipment

main muscle helpers

Step by step

  1. Stand tall and hold a wall for balance.
  2. Rise up onto your tiptoes as high as you can.
  3. Lower your heels slowly back down.

💡 Tip: Stand on a step and let your heels drop below it to work harder.
