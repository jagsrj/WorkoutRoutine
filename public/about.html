Questions 🙋

Is KinFit really free?

Yes. Every plan, exercise and feature is free, with no ads and no sign-up.

Is it safe for kids?

Kids under 13 always start on bodyweight exercises. Child profiles get simpler, kid-safe Coach answers with no diet or weight-loss advice, and a parent PIN locks important settings. Under-18s should lift weights with an adult or coach nearby.

Do I need a gym?

No. The Beginner plan needs only a wall, a chair and a backpack. If you have home equipment, KinFit shows the version of each exercise you can do with it.

Where is my data?

On your phone. You can turn on an optional backup protected by a private recovery code, with no account or email. Progress photos never leave your phone. See the privacy policy.

Is there an app to download?

KinFit runs in your browser. On iPhone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen and it opens like an app, even offline.
